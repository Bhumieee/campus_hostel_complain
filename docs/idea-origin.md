# Idea Origin & Problem Statement

## 1. Executive Context & Problem Statement
On university and institutional campuses, physical infrastructure (lab computers, classroom projectors, air conditioners, plumbing, and furniture) deteriorates constantly. Despite dedicated facilities management teams, traditional reporting systems fail due to key operational bottlenecks:

* **High Friction & Reporting Barriers:** Complicated administrative portals require multi-step logins, lengthy web forms, and manual identification of building, room, and equipment numbers—discouraging immediate, on-the-spot reporting.
* **The "Black Hole" Effect (Lack of Transparency):** Students and faculty submit complaints without visibility into ticket progress, assigned personnel, or estimated resolution times, leading to frustration and apathy.
* **Duplicate Effort & Cluttered Queues:** Dozens of individuals report the same broken projector or washroom tap separately, overloading administrative staff with redundant entries.
